How ya going?
/haʊ ə ˈɡoʊɪŋ/ — “how-uh-GOH-in”
Practise it →
🇦🇺 Aussie“How ya going?” — friendly, quick, linked together
🇺🇸 Standard“How are you doing?” — slower, every word clear

Common mistake: pronouncing every word separately. Aussies glide “ya” and “going” together.
